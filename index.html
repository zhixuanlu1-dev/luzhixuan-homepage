卢知轩，厦门大学理学博士，华侨大学发光研究院/材料学院副研究员，硕士生导师。Dr. Zhixuan Lu received his PhD in Science from Xiamen University and is currently an Associate Researcher at the Institute of Luminescent Materials and Information Displays / College of Materials Science and Engineering, Huaqiao University.

以第一或通讯作者在 ACS Energy Letters、ACS Catalysis、Analytical Chemistry、Chemical Engineering Journal、Biosensors and Bioelectronics、Nanoscale 等国际权威期刊发表 SCI 论文 13 篇，累计发表 SCI 论文 20 余篇；主持国家自然科学基金青年项目、福建省创青项目、中国博士后科学基金面上项目、固体表面物理化学国家重点实验室开放课题及华侨大学科研启动项目等项目。As first or corresponding author, he has published 13 SCI papers in leading journals including ACS Energy Letters, ACS Catalysis, Analytical Chemistry, Chemical Engineering Journal, Biosensors and Bioelectronics, and Nanoscale, with more than 20 SCI papers in total. He leads projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (Young Scientists Fund), Fujian Province, the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, the State Key Laboratory of Physical Chemistry of Solid Surfaces, and Huaqiao University.

目前研究方向为等离激元纳米结构的制备及其在钙钛矿发光领域的应用。招收材料、化学等相关专业方向的硕士研究生，同时欢迎优秀本科生进组进行科研训练。指导本科生获得2025年省级创新创业大赛铜奖和2026年国家级大学生创新训练项目。His current research focuses on the preparation of plasmonic nanostructures and their applications in perovskite luminescence. He recruits master’s students in materials science, chemistry, and related fields, and welcomes outstanding undergraduates to join the group for research training. He has mentored undergraduate students who won a Bronze Prize at the 2025 provincial innovation and entrepreneurship competition and were awarded a 2026 National Undergraduate Innovation Training Program.

研究方向Research Interests

✦

钙钛矿材料及其在发光二极管和传感中应用Perovskite Materials for LEDs and Sensing

钙钛矿纳米晶/量子点的合成、稳定性调控与光电性能研究，及其在高效（纯蓝光）发光二极管和离子/生物传感中的应用。Synthesis, stability engineering, and optoelectronic properties of perovskite nanocrystals/quantum dots, and their applications in high-efficiency (pure-blue) light-emitting diodes and ion/biosensing.

◎

等离激元纳米材料及其在光谱电化学和发光二极管中应用Plasmonic Nanomaterials for Spectroelectrochemistry & LEDs

等离激元纳米材料（Au、Ag、AuAg 合金及核壳结构）的可控制备与表面等离激元共振（SPR）调控，及其在原位光谱电化学与发光二极管机理研究中的应用。Controlled synthesis of plasmonic nanomaterials (Au, Ag, AuAg alloys and core–shell designs) with tunable surface plasmon resonance (SPR), and their applications in in-situ spectroelectrochemistry and light-emitting diode mechanism studies.
